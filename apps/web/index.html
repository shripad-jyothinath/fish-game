❓ How To Play

🎮 Movement & Controls: Steer with your Mouse Cursor, or use WASD / Arrow Keys (or touch joystick on mobile).

⚡ Turbo Boost: Hold Spacebar, Shift, or Left Click (or touch BOOST button) for an explosive speed dash attack!

🗡️ Slice & Survive: Use your snout-mounted sword to stab opponents from behind or flank their sides to slice them into sushi!

🍣 Eat & Grow: Swallow floating sushi and defeated fish meat to level up, increase your size, and lengthen your sword reach.

⭐ Power-ups & Treasure: Collect Bubble Shields (🛡️), Magnets (🧲), Speed Stars (⭐), and crack open Sunken Chests (💎) for massive gold!

⚔️ Blade Parry: If two blades clash directly head-to-head, they bounce off with a parry spark!

👑 Level Challenges & Bosses: Progress through 15 stages to slay the Giant Megalodon and the Golden Dragon Leviathan!
